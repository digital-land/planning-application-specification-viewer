Field

Extension limits exceeded

Whether a proposed extension exceeds any of the listed Class Q extension limits, or no extension is proposed.

Reference
cls-q-extension-exceeded
Name
Extension limits exceeded
Datatype
enum
Cardinality

(Number expected)

1
Codelist
yes-no-not-applicable
Entry date
2026-10-18
Notes

Will any proposed extension:

  • be more than 1 storey;
  • be sited anywhere other than the rear of existing building;
  • extend beyond the rear wall of existing building by more than 4 metres;
  • extend beyond side or principal elevations of the existing building;
  • create eaves higher than the height of the current building's eaves;
  • be higher than the highest part of the roof of the existing building, or exceed 4 metres in height;
  • be on land that is not already covered by a hard surface, with that hard surface having existed on or before 24th July 2023 or, where the hard surface was provided after 24th July 2023, has been in place for at least 10 years before development begins.

Record yes if any listed limit is exceeded, no if an extension is proposed but none is exceeded, or not-applicable if no extension is proposed. The source form combines “No / No extension proposed” in one answer option; a tick in that option alone cannot distinguish the two states.

Source: section 4a of the Class Q application form, version ECAB 2024.2.
